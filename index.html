Why CrewTap Outperforms Traditional Hiring

Built on Cloudflare Workers edge nodes and Supabase Postgres atomic functions, delivering zero downtime and zero double-assignments.

🌊

Wave-Alert Dispatch

Jobs aren't broadcast blindly to hundreds of phones. CrewTap alerts pre-screened local workers in staggered waves of 5, prioritizing rapid responders while honoring WhatsApp 24h free windows.

🔒

Atomic Postgres Claiming

Our custom SQL claim_job() engine with row-level locks ensures that when you need 4 workers, exactly 4 workers are assigned. Zero race conditions, even during high-concurrency peak hours.

Native Trilingual Bot

Built with native localization in English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी). Workers interact in their mother tongue with clear yes/no buttons, eliminating literacy friction.
